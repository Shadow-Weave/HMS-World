INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - NIGHT

Mira, a detective in a dark coat, studies a brass lantern on a desk. In a quiet
close shot she turns the lantern's hidden latch and its blue light comes alive.
The light reveals a remembered photograph pinned on the archive wall. A courier,
Ivo in a green coat, enters after the glow and reaches for the lantern; Mira keeps
it between them. The final image returns to the earlier wall photograph as Mira
realizes it identifies the courier's destination.
